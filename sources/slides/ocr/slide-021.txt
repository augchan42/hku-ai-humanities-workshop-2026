should criticism end in silence?

Pyrrhonism: When competing considerations appear equally persuasive, or concepts are
deconstructed, judgement is suspended. Result: Tranquility of some kind.

Arguments expose difficulties in our commitments or concepts; confrontation with those
dissolves the demand for metaphysical answers.

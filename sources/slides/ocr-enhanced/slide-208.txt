NECESSARY CONDITIONS AND THE GAME OF LACKS

Objection: Text alone cannot provide grounding

L

The grounding objection says that an LLM encounters symbols rather than the things those symbols

concern.

But those symbols are produced through human interactions with the world. The training corpus has a

causal history connecting it to objects and events.

We can think about Plato without having encountered him. We can talk about places we have never

visited.

Externalist theories help explain those capacities.

The absence of direct acquaintance doesn’t establish the absence of reference.

Our Question, Conceptual Engineering and Apt Methodology
Perhaps what you say is true about understanding, say, in its fu// human version. But the crucial
question that needs answering is not Q but rather Q*:

(Q*) Do Als instantiate the bare essence of these typical human capacities? It
might be interesting to ask whether LLMs are able to instantiate the full human
version of, say, understanding, but it’s even more important to ask whether they
are capable of wnderstanding per se, even if it’s not in its full human version — the
bare essence of understanding, as we may put it.

(Q**) Do they instantiate concepts of understanding that are specially engineered to
suit the new kind of intelligence they represent?

However, I would insist that the most responsible and fruitful way to approach these issues is to
start with asking about the full human case, that is, to start with Q itself.

The Main Arguments for the Opposing Views
Our Question has quickly become very polarizing.

Believers are convinced that LLMs understand, know, believe, desire, intend and reason (Hinton,
Cappelen, Sutskever, Lederman). Skeptics (Marcus, Bender, Chomsky, Pearl, Pinker, Penrose)
who are just as convinced that LLMs ate simply very sophisticated, very fast pattern-matchers.

In favor of the Believer’s thesis that LLMs have human-like mental capacities there is first and /
foremost the fact that they are so zmpressive at what they can do. Best explanation is mentalistic.

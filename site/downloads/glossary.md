# Words, unpacked

Editorial definitions for this workshop.

## LLM

A large language model: an AI trained to work with sequences of text pieces. The workshop asks what abilities its behaviour gives us reason to attribute to it.

PDF pages: 5, 6.

## Token

A piece of text processed by a language model. It can be a word, part of a word, or punctuation.

PDF pages: 5.

## Phenomenology

What an experience feels like from the inside—for example, the feeling of a headache.

PDF pages: 1.

## Phenomenal consciousness

Having experiences with a felt character. This is narrower than being able to talk about experiences.

PDF pages: 1, 9.

## Epistemology

Questions about what we know and how we could know it.

PDF pages: 2.

## Metaphysics

Questions about what exists and what kinds of things those things are.

PDF pages: 2, 3.

## Private-language argument

A challenge to the idea that a language could get its meaning entirely from private inner checks, with no shared standard for correct use. Its force and interpretation are disputed in the handouts.

PDF pages: 2.

## Open texture

Room for judgment when a familiar word meets a new kind of case that its established use does not clearly settle.

PDF pages: 1.

## Pragmatism

In Sharadin’s discussion, an approach that explains a kind of language by asking what using it does in our lives.

PDF pages: 3, 4.

## Intentional stance

Predicting and explaining something by treating it as having beliefs and goals.

PDF pages: 3.

## Interpretationism / interpretivism

A family of views connecting having beliefs and desires with being appropriately interpretable as having them. The speakers disagree about what makes an interpretation appropriate and what it establishes.

PDF pages: 6.

## Intentionality

The ‘aboutness’ of a thought or other mental state—for example, a belief about tomorrow’s weather. Here it does not just mean planning to act.

PDF pages: 6.

## Grounding

The connection between symbols and what they are about. The concern is how words get beyond connections to other words.

PDF pages: 6.

## Derivative meaning

Meaning that depends on a relationship with other meaning-makers, rather than originating entirely in the current user of a symbol.

PDF pages: 6.

## Conceptual engineering

Deliberately revising a concept to make it more useful or suitable for a purpose.

PDF pages: 5.

## Inference to the best explanation

Concluding that something is likely because it best explains the evidence, rather than observing it directly.

PDF pages: 7.

## Grasp of a rule

Understanding a general method in a way that guides new applications. Boghossian asks what distinguishes this from producing a good series of answers.

PDF pages: 8, 11.

## Justificatory requirement

Boghossian’s requirement that understanding provide a reason for an action, as well as help cause or explain it.

PDF pages: 8, 9, 12.

## Infinitary

Extending over infinitely many possible cases, such as addition for every pair of natural numbers.

PDF pages: 8, 12.

## Plus/quus problem

The puzzle that a finite record of answers can fit addition and rival rules that diverge only in untested cases.

PDF pages: 8, 12.

## Grokking

In the handout, the shift from apparently memorizing training examples to performing well on new cases by learning something more general.

PDF pages: 11, 12.

## Modulo 97

Arithmetic that wraps around after 96. This creates a finite task, unlike ordinary addition over all natural numbers.

PDF pages: 12.

## Moral standing

Counting morally in your own right, so that how you are treated matters for your own sake.

PDF pages: 3, 4.

## Quasi-realism

The approach Sharadin draws from Blackburn: explain the practical role of a kind of language while preserving its ordinary serious claims, rather than treating them as useful make-believe.

PDF pages: 4.

## Existential orientation

A broad outlook or way of relating to the world. The handouts dispute whether extending consciousness language to AI ultimately belongs here.

PDF pages: 1, 3, 4.

## Embodiment

Having a body through which a being senses and acts in its surroundings.

PDF pages: 2, 3, 6.
